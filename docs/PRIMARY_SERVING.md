# Primary serving and background research (6 November 2026)

The former all-in-one workflow is now two jobs: primary and research. Primary collects date-causal history, prepares/replays unchanged inputs, builds opponent features from the existing ID-safe ledger and bounded team enrichment, calls the ORIGINAL hash-locked inference function, freezes legacy and complete-market choices, renders main HTML, saves an encrypted primary checkpoint and publishes just the canonical private HTML immediately. Research consumes that exact checkpoint and previous journals on another runner, trains its own arms, refreshes outcomes and exports full evaluations later. A research failure does not remove the already saved main HTML.

No neural/statistical fit is called in primary serving. All fitted weights, medians/transforms, score distribution/covariance and model/pipeline hashes stay frozen. Main-only enrichment no longer performs research-only historical training identity lookups; current provider identities and previous ledger remain available. This can change context COVERAGE compared to the old expensive development build; missing values retain locked preprocessing, never fabricated IDs. Current features are frozen at the earlier serving timestamp. Research cannot retrospectively change them or main predictions. Source context/feature/ledger inputs are retained separately under primary-context-*; stability snapshot records those paths and its original context digest. All are encrypted, and their hashes are control-protected.

Private panel gets raporlar/YYYY-MM-DD/futbol.html and basketbol.html plus primary-ready.json. The readiness record points to primary-forecasts-YYYY-MM-DD encrypted artifact in the in-progress run and says research/outcome refresh is pending. It is NOT a successful final evaluation manifest. The full checkpoint-manifest and evaluations arrive after research success. Existing ready/final daily outputs are not replaced by a second primary prediction. Controls restore original forecast bytes and refresh results in research. The final private exporter replaces primary HTML with the SAME forecast's refreshed results.

This does not remove the HTTP history cost: fresh date-causal history remains necessary before serving. On the observed 6 November run history collection took 30m08s, while models/research/settlement after replay cost roughly 24m. Expect readiness after history + replay + bounded enrichment/inference/render/upload, not an unmeasured 5-minute claim. The production coordinator's preceding-day control still preserves settled training journals before dispatch. No change to Telegram or cron/Daily/Control data scraping.

Recovery boundaries: primary-forecasts are encrypted checkpoints and primary-ready is early readiness only. It must never be silently treated as a successful full evaluation artifact. The existing strict failed-source recovery allowlists remain unchanged. If background research fails, inspect that source and its preserved forecasts before any retry; never issue replacements for the same date.
